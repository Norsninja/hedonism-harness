## Strategic state at v0.42 handoff

v0.42 is the **first causal probe** in the science arc. The
post-hoc reducer arc (v0.34..v0.41) established a correlational
pattern: the tick-50 leader lineage is associated with post-50
dominance across 4 of 5 seed streams.

v0.42 tested the narrow hypothesis that the tick-50 leader is
**necessary** for post-50 dominance, by hard-killing the leader
at the tick-50/tick-51 boundary and observing whether the pattern
fails to reconstitute. **The pattern reconstitutes through another
surviving lineage.** A size-matched non-leader removal also does
not collapse the pattern.

The locked verdict is:

> v0.42 rules out the tick-50 leader lineage as a **necessary
> causal carrier of post-50 dominance under this hard-kill
> intervention.** The dominance pattern reconstitutes through
> another surviving lineage, suggesting the causal source is
> more likely substrate-level than leader-identity-level.

What this does NOT establish: spatial position, energy stockpile,
founder traits, local food control, and descendants-already-
distributed are not ruled out as causal forces *in general*. They
may still be active mechanisms — but they are **replaceable /
niche-refillable** under hard kill, not bound to the original
leader's identity. The kill proves the pattern doesn't depend on
the *specific* leader; it does not prove these substrate-anchored
properties are not the mechanism.

The right scientific posture: **the signal survives leader
removal, so the causal search should pivot from lineage identity
to substrate structure.**

The locked v0.43 candidate (single substrate intervention, one at
a time for clean reads): **(g) resource concentration shock /
resource flattening.** Hazard relocation, chamber geometry sweeps,
and cross-stream calibration of v0.42 are deferred to v0.44+.

The post-hoc reducer arc remains correlationally valid; the
causal arc has its first negative result, sharpening the next
question.

HedonismPolicy and Mesa remain deferred indefinitely.
