- 40 seeds per hazard (v0.25 1..8 + v0.32 9..16 + v0.33 17..24 +

Strict ordering required by the audit's input dependencies:

- v0.41 audit reads the sealed outputs of v0.34, v0.35, v0.38 (OLD)
  AND v0.34/v0.35 fresh-v0.39 extensions AND v0.39 reducer per_run
  AND the THREE new v0.41 extensions. All must complete first.
- v0.41 v0.35 extension anchors against v0.41 v0.34 extension output.
- v0.41 v0.38 extension anchors against v0.41 v0.34 + v0.35 outputs.
- v0.41 audit is independent of v0.36 (trait_replay), v0.37
  (lock_in_timing_replay), and v0.40 audit — does not read those
  outputs.

If any prior reducer's output is missing, v0.41 audit halts via
`LineageReplayError` ("sealed CSV missing at ...").

## Operational notes

- All reducer outputs are pure functions of the on-disk sidecars +
  per-arm `comparison.csv`. Re-running any reducer over the same
  corpus is idempotent.
- v0.41 audit is **idempotent** because it reads sealed CSVs only.
  Re-running v0.41 audit produces byte-identical outputs.
- A failed re-anchor or invariant is **always** the right behaviour.
  Investigate; do NOT bypass the halt.
- Wall-time budget for full regen + all reducers including v0.41:
  ~106s sweeps + ~17s OLD/FRESH-v0.39 reducers + ~2s v0.40 audit +
  ~4s v0.41 extensions + ~2s v0.41 audit ≈ **~131s** end-to-end.
