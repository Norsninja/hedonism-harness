## Where we left off

v0.22 shipped a major substantive surprise: **recycling was a net tax,
not a fuel.** Arm A (hazard=0) on food_ladder produced b>50=116 — 26%
*above* the v0.21 saturation plateau (92). Productivity strictly
*decreases* with hazard_damage: 116 → 96 → 92 → 65. Pre-registered
band 4 ("injury-deaths-as-cull") fires unambiguously. The v0.21
food_ladder 92-plateau was a hazard-constrained productivity plateau
with recycling subsidy, not a pure geometric ceiling. This **opened a
new question**: what mechanism produces the v0.21 1.0/tick chamber
asymmetry now that recycling-as-fuel is falsified? v0.23 pre-reg is
drafted to answer it (rerun v0.21's 5-point influx frontier at
hazard_damage=0 on **both** chambers, 80 runs total). No code
written yet for v0.23.

## Decisions locked in

- **Recycling-as-net-tax confirmed.** v0.22 band 4 fires; v0.21's
  92-b>50 plateau was hazard-cull-supplemented, not a hard
  geometric ceiling. The cull was the binding force.
- **v0.21 chamber-asymmetry mechanism is now mechanism-unknown.**
  Three candidates — geometry/food-accessibility, hazard-interaction
  artifact, tight-was-hazard-wall-constrained. v0.23 isolates them.
- **v0.23 design pre-committed.** 5 arms × 2 chambers × 8 seeds = 80
  runs; hazard_damage=0 fixed; ambient_influx_rate ∈ {0, 0.5, 1.0,
  1.5, 2.0}; transfer mode; pool=1500; same v0.21/v0.22 seeds.
  Three gap-bands (≥+0.5 / 0–<+0.5 / ≤−0.5) + three independent
  tight-i\* bands (≥1.5 / 1.0–1.4 / ≤1.0).
- **Branch hygiene change vs prior cadence.** v0.22 reused the
  session-assigned branch (`claude/hedonism-harness-0.22-4gshB`) per
  user pushback against churn; v0.23 cuts a fresh branch
  (`claude/v0.23-hazard-zero-frontier`) per user direction in this
  session. Going forward, expect fresh per-version branches unless
  user calls otherwise.
- **Operational i\* definitions for v0.23 are self-referential** —
  primary i\* = lowest influx where b>50 ≥ 90% of *same chamber's*
  arm-E b>50 (not a v0.21 prior). Secondary i\* same on
  total_births. 90% threshold preserved from v0.21.

## Watch-outs

- **H5/H6 are hard pins.** Every v0.23 arm must produce
  `injury_deaths=0` AND `pool_in_death_residual=0`. The sweep
  driver should display both columns; any non-zero value is a
  halt condition (mechanism regression).
- **Tight chamber at hazard=0 has no prior anchor.** First
  observation; no byte-identity check available on tight. Only
  the food_ladder C anchor (vs v0.22) catches wiring drift.
- **Hazard avoidance behavior is dormant at hazard=0.** The
  gradient policy senses `hazard_damage` directly; with damage=0,
  the avoidance signal is zero and agents enter hazard tiles
  freely. v0.22 hazard-0 had hazard_entries=200 (vs 58 at
  hazard=12). On tight, this could change action dynamics
  meaningfully — agents that previously routed around the
  hazard wall now path through it.
- **Arm A on tight (influx=0, hazard=0) may extinct.** v0.22
  hazard-0 food_ladder did NOT extinct at influx=1.0 (8/8 surv),
  but tight at influx=0 with no hazard cull is an unobserved
  regime. The v0.23 pre-reg's H1 explicitly accommodates per-seed
  early termination via the `executed_ticks_per_seed` formulation;
  the sweep aggregator + driver must annotate
  seeds-with-early-termination cleanly.
- **fcpb interpretation under hazard=0 is mode-shifted.** Under
  transfer mode the per-birth system-energy is 30 (gap-only).
  v0.22 arm A hit fcpb=53.82 — near the metabolic floor for
  sustainability (≈60 if half of food becomes child body). v0.23
  arms with high productivity (low influx, hazard=0) may dip
  below this, which is informative not a failure — but the
  pre-reg keeps fcpb as observational not pinned.
- **Determinism north star: `scripts/core_smoke_test.py` passed
  at handoff.** All three stages (pure-core RNG hash; same-seed
  bit-identity; different-seed divergence) green at v0.22 merge.

  remote branches between sessions. Local cleanup is sufficient
  on this side. The merged v0.22 branch
  `claude/hedonism-harness-0.22-4gshB` was deleted locally this
  session; the remote pruning is on the user.
- **PRs are squash-merged.** Reach into the merged squash

- **v0.23 design has 15 hypotheses (H1..H15)** vs v0.22's 14 —
