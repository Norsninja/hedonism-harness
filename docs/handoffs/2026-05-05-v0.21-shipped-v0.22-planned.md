## Decisions locked in

- **Strict-transfer realised at the system-ledger level.** Per-agent body deltas (parent −15, child +30) are mode-invariant; only the pool debit shrinks (30 → 15) and the `reproduction_heat_loss` vs `parent_energy_transferred_to_child` accumulators differ. `core/reproduction.py` is **unchanged**. This was the load-bearing implementation insight — keeps v0.19 bit-identity automatic and isolates the mechanism to one branch in `_process_birth_queue`.
- **POOL_FULL is the default and preserves v0.19 bit-identity by construction.** New branches guard behind the mode flag; the parent-energy gate fires only under TRANSFER (preserving v0.19's `charge_parent` floor-at-zero contract).
- **`offspring_start_energy >= energy_cost` validator is mode-conditional.** Under POOL_FULL the v0.7..v0.19 bare default (cost=35, offspring=30) remains legal — heat-loss bookkeeping does not require non-negative gap. Required only under TRANSFER. Bare-default tests would break otherwise.
- **TRANSFER mode rejects inf-pool** at `HHModel.__init__`. No silent degradation; explicit ValueError. Bit-identity anchor moved to "POOL_FULL=False reproduces v0.19" (per-arm, not per-config).
- **fcpb≥45 floor retired under TRANSFER.** Floor is a POOL_FULL concept (heat + pool startup = 45/birth). Under TRANSFER the per-birth burden is 30 (gap-only). Reported as observational throughput metric, not as a fixed criterion. POOL_FULL arms keep the v0.19 floor.
- **H7 sharp transition is operationalised** as "one consecutive-arm step accounts for >50% of the (E−A) total-blocks delta." Primary i\* = lowest influx with b>50 ≥ 90% of arm E b>50; secondary i\* = same with total_births. 90% threshold is lenient on integer-rounded counts but still meaningful.
- **Byte-identity vs semantic-regression anchor distinction.** Byte-identity = full event stream + aggregates match. Semantic regression = aggregates match; event stream not required. v0.20 transfer-open-low tight had 3 r_blk + 9 b_blk timing perturbation → semantic regression on tight, byte-identity on food_ladder. Distinction must be pre-committed in any future pre-reg.
- **v0.22 narrowed to hazard_damage sweep**, not broad chamber-geometry parameterisation. The zero-hazard arm is the crucial recycling-vs-geometry control.
