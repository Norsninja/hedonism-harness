## Decisions locked in

- **v0.27 food_ladder dip explanation closed.** The w=0.75 dip is a 2-of-8 seed concentration (v0.28); does not reproduce on a fresh seed stream (v0.29). Future references should frame it as "sample-stream-specific late-birth contraction on seeds 1..8."
- **v0.27 "food_ladder interior optimum at w=0.5" demoted from mechanism to sample-noise.** The +6 b>50 reverses on seeds 9..16. Safer claim: "at 8 seeds, food_ladder productivity in w=0.5–1.0 is mostly weight-insensitive, with small aggregate differences that can invert by seed stream."
- **What survives across both streams:** routing channel responds reliably to weight (hazard entries / injury deaths / starvation deaths all monotone non-increasing); productivity channel non-monotone/noisy in this band; most individual seeds nearly insensitive across w ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1.0}.
- **Methodological rule for v0.30+ (the real upgrade from v0.29):** *At 8 seeds, do not promote small aggregate optima into mechanisms unless they reproduce across a fresh seed stream or have strong per-seed consistency.*
- **`run_diagnostic(DiagnosticConfig)` is the canonical reusable entrypoint** for any per-seed paired-comparison diagnostic going forward. v0.30 uses it directly (no new analysis surface needed).
